    Safety Rules & Regulations

    Mandatory Compliance

    1. Pre-Flight Requirements

    Before every flight, you must:

    • Check weather conditions and forecast
    • Inspect drone for damage or malfunctions
    • Verify battery levels and equipment status
    • Obtain necessary permits and permissions
    • Conduct a risk assessment of the flight area
    • File flight plans with authorities when required

    2. During Flight Rules

    During operation, you must:

    • Maintain visual line of sight at all times
    • Respect maximum altitude limits (typically 400 feet/120m)
    • Keep safe distance from people, vehicles, and structures
    • Avoid flying near airports (minimum 5 miles/8km)
    • Monitor airspace restrictions using official apps
    • Yield right of way to manned aircraft
    • Have a pre-planned emergency landing site

    3. Post-Flight Procedures

    After each flight, you must:

    • Log flight details including duration and location
    • Report any incidents or near-misses
    • Properly store and maintain equipment
    • Charge batteries according to manufacturer guidelines
    • Update maintenance records
    • Securely store collected data

    4. Emergency Procedures

    In case of emergency:

    • Activate return-to-home function immediately
    • Move to a safe location if drone approaches
    • Contact local authorities if there's property damage or injury
    • Report incidents to DRONO within 24 hours
    • Preserve all flight data and logs
